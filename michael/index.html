§ 01 · The Feature

From Michael.

A note from the wrestler

PAUSAW State Tournament · Medalist

"Fargo is the biggest tournament of the year for high school wrestlers my age — and only a handful from each state even qualify."

This past spring I earned a spot on the Pennsylvania National Team for Greco-Roman wrestling, which means I get to compete at the High School National Championships as a 16U qualifier in Fargo, North Dakota, July 13–15. Fargo is the biggest tournament of the year for high school wrestlers my age — the best kids in every state are there, and only a handful from each state even qualify for it, which is why I am so excited having earned a spot.

This will be my first time qualifying for the trip to Fargo, and I know it is going to be among the most incredible experiences I have ever had in my life. I'm currently an 8th grader at Shaler Area, and will begin my Freshman year in HS in the fall.

Before Fargo, Team PA has a training camp at Penn West Clarion (July 5–7), then we travel together as a team to North Dakota and compete July 13–15. Between team gear, camp, travel, room and board, and entry fees, the total cost is around $1,500. That's a lot for my family to cover on top of everything else they already do for my wrestling, so I'm trying to raise as much as I can on my own.

Pick a number that speaks to you — a jersey number, a birthday, a lucky number. Whatever it is. I'll remember it. — M.C., 138 GR

A close friend and Shaler/PA teammate helped me set up a fun "block" fundraiser to get me started. There are 60 blocks below, numbered 1 through 60. You pick a block, you donate that dollar amount. Block 5 = $5. Block 42 = $42. Block 60 = $60. When every block is claimed, I'll have raised $1,830 — enough for the trip plus a little cushion.

Every single block matters to me. Whether you can take #3 or #58, I'll be extremely grateful, and I'll remember it. Thank you for believing in me, and for being part of representing my school, my city, and my community on the National stage.

— Michael

§ 04 · Background

Why it matters.

Fargo · Team Pennsylvania
★ Fargo, ND · July 13–15

The largest age-group tournament in the United States.

The USA Wrestling 16U & Junior National Championships — known to the wrestling world simply as "Fargo" — is where every state sends its top qualifying athletes. All-American honors (top 8) from Fargo are among the most coveted credentials in high school wrestling and weigh heavily in college recruiting.

★ Team Pennsylvania

Six straight National Duals titles.

Wrestlers earn their spot on the Pennsylvania National Team by placing at the PAUSAW State Tournament or by being a current or past All-American at a USA Wrestling national event. PA's 16U Boys team has won the Freestyle National Duals title six years running. Joining this team is recognition as one of the top wrestlers in the state.
